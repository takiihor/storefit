General rules

  • 01Images must not contain an alpha channel or transparent pixels.
  • 02Dimensions must match exactly. This tool exports exact pixel sizes.
  • 03Each screenshot size supports up to 10 images. Check Apple documentation for device scaling rules.
  • 04The 5244 × 2950 targets use PNG automatically, even when JPEG is selected.
  • 05Headers and search assets may be cropped. Center key subjects and text with safe margins.
  • 06Exports use sRGB. Confirm that you can use the fonts, trademarks, and text in your assets.
